How the classes work

  • Understand the process

    Every topic starts with the why: how a cell, an organ or a pathway really works, before any definition is learned.

  • Draw it and label it

    Diagrams carry a large share of the marks in biology, so every class includes drawing and labelling practice.

  • Test what stuck

    Short quizzes and past-paper questions show what you know and what needs another look.
